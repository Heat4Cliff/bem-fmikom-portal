## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system embodies the identity of a modern, forward-thinking university student executive board. It balances institutional credibility with youth-driven energy: authoritative yet approachable, structured yet dynamic, intellectual yet inspiring. The interface evokes feelings of trust, progressive leadership, academic excellence, and collegiate vitality.

### Design Movement: Modern Academic & Contemporary Corporate
The visual aesthetic merges Contemporary Corporate SaaS structure with editorial academic warmth. 
- **Public Profile:** Editorial whitespace, strong typographic scales, structured card grids, warm golden highlights against midnight foundations, and expressive leadership presentation.
- **Administrative Portal:** High-density utilitarian layouts, crisp data framing, minimal extraneous decor, and rapid scanning capabilities.

All viewports adhere strictly to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards, delivering high readability across daylight and campus environments without sacrificing elegance.

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Role Distribution
- **Primary (`#1E3A8A` - Royal Navy):** Anchors brand authority. Used for primary CTAs, active navigation states, primary headings on public layouts, and dominant interactive elements.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#D97706` & `#F59E0B` - Warm Amber/Gold):** Represents prestige, dynamic intellect, and student optimism. Used deliberately for focal highlights, status badges, celebratory milestones, active indicators, and secondary accent touches. Never overused as large background fills.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Serves as deep grounding for text, administrative sidebars, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - `Canvas / Background`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) creates a soft, glare-free canvas.
  - `Card / Surface Default`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - `Surface Muted / Neutral Subdued`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100).
  - `Border / Divider Line`: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200).
  - `Text Muted / Subtitle`: `#64748B` (Slate 500).
  - `Text Body`: `#334155` (Slate 700).
  - `Text Headings`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900).

### Semantic Indicators
- **Success (`#10B981` Emerald):** Status completed, approved submissions, active terms.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B` Amber):** Pending reviews, approaching deadlines, warnings.
- **Danger (`#EF4444` Rose):** Form invalidations, rejected approvals, destructive actions.
- **Info (`#0284C7` Sky):** System notices, information disclaimers, agenda markers.

## Typography

### Font Pair Strategy
- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric structure softened by humanist subtleties. It conveys contemporary energy without looking playful or immature, ideal for student organizational leadership.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`):** Selected for maximum legibility in dense academic tables, descriptive documents, and administrative toolbars. Its neutral x-height ensures strain-free reading.

### Scaling & Rules
- Headings display crisp negative letter-spacing to reinforce authority and cohesion.
- Mobile breakpoints switch automatically to `-mobile` tokens for `display` and `headline-xl` to prevent awkward word wraps.
- Text links inherit font weight `500` or `600` with subtle underlines or amber hover states.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Public Website:** Fluid 12-column grid capped at a maximum container width of `1280px` (`80rem`). Centers content with balanced generous section margins.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Rigid multi-panel architecture. Fixed sidebar (`260px` collapsed to `72px`), dynamic fluid content canvas with minimal max-width restriction, enabling full tabular data rendering.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Section padding drops to `space-lg`. Table rows collapse into structured key-value cards. Top nav switches to full-height drawer.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar switches to off-canvas modal; grids convert from 3-column cards to 2-column cards.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout. Permanent sidebar navigation, multi-column dashboard statistics, and full contextual filter toolbars.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Depth Strategy
This design system avoids harsh, unblended drop shadows. Hierarchy is achieved through a combination of crisp container borders (`#E2E8F0`) and diffused slate-tinted ambient shadows.

### Layer Tiers
- **Flat (Level 0):** Background surfaces (`#F8FAFC`), structural canvas dividers, and table row stripes.
- **Subtle (Level 1 - `shadow-sm`):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Applied to standard cards, administrative form inputs, list items, and basic table containers, paired with a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Elevated (Level 2 - `shadow-md`):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Applied to interactive cards on hover, sticky navigation headers, and standard dropdowns.
- **Floating (Level 3 - `shadow-lg`):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Applied to modals, slide-over panels, popover menus, and command palettes.

Admin tables favor border segregation over elevation to maintain high data density.

## Shapes

### Corner Geometry
The system uses **Rounded (Tier 2)** styling:
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`):** Buttons, text input boxes, dropdown selectors, badges, and table header frames. Provides a clean, functional silhouette.
- **Primary Cards & Containers (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Content cards, department organization boards, data-table containers, and modal dialogs.
- **Hero & Feature Banners (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Landing page showcase sections, media framing, and event callout banners.
- **Pill Elements (`9999px` / `rounded-full`):** Category tags, count indicators, avatar circles, and pill action toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Navy (`#1E3A8A`) background, white text, subtle hover shift to `#1E293B`, 0.5rem radius, medium weight typography. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#F59E0B`.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Warm Amber (`#D97706`), white text, hover to `#B45309`. Reserved for standout conversion points (e.g., "Daftar Aspirasi", "Registrasi Kegiatan").
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), slate text (`#334155`), background transparent. Hover transforms to `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`), font size `label-sm`.
- Department badges use neutral tones: `#F1F5F9` background with `#1E293B` text.
- Status badges use semantic soft backgrounds with bold text (e.g., Emerald soft `#ECFDF5` with `#065F46` label).

### Cards
- **Public Profile Cards:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-lg` (1rem), subtle shadow (`shadow-sm`). On hover, translate Y by `-2px` with transition to `shadow-md` and an optional gold accent border indicator on the top edge.
- **Admin Metric Cards:** High visual compactness, crisp border, numeric indicator in display font, subtle icon badge on top right.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input heights standard `40px` (desktop admin) and `44px` (touch public).
- Base border `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#1E3A8A` with a subtle amber shadow aura (`0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`).
- Checkboxes and radios styled in primary navy with white checkmark glyphs.

### Admin Data Tables & Toolbars
- **Header:** Sticky positioning, background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-sm` font in `#64748B`, bottom border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Row:** Height `48px`, alternating subtle hover state `#F8FAFC`, action menus anchored right.
- **Toolbar:** Integrated search input, department filter pills, date-range picker, and export action grouped in a singular responsive bar.

### Department & Organizational Hierarchy Cards
- Specialized card featuring profile imagery, cabinet division title tag, member name, and direct academic contact badges.
